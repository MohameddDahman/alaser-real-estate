# Photography requirement

**Status: blocking.** The client has no photography. This document is the brief
for the first shoot.

The site is designed so it does not collapse without photographs — an
unphotographed unit renders its own measured plan in the exact aspect box a
photograph will occupy. That is a deliberate holding position, not a
substitute. Nothing on this site is stock, and nothing should become stock:
interchangeable stock imagery is the single loudest signal that a property site
is templated, and it is the first thing a buyer in Faisal recognises.

---

## Per unit — the minimum set

Twelve frames. Below this a listing does not convert.

| # | Frame | Why it earns its place |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Building exterior from the street | Buyers judge the building before the flat. Show the entrance and the street it sits on. |
| 2 | Entrance / lobby, including the lift | "Is there a lift, does it work" is a real question. |
| 3 | Reception (صالة), wide, from the door | The room the whole sale turns on. Shoot from the entrance, the way a viewer first sees it. |
| 4 | Reception, reverse angle | Shows the real depth. One angle always flatters. |
| 5 | Kitchen, showing the counter run | Cabinets in or not in is a five-figure difference. |
| 6 | Master bedroom | |
| 7 | Second bedroom | |
| 8 | Third bedroom (if any) | Every bedroom, every time. A missing bedroom reads as a hidden problem. |
| 9 | Main bathroom | |
| 10 | **View from the main balcony, facing out** | This is what they are buying. Shoot the actual view, not the balcony floor. |
| 11 | The balcony itself, looking back in | Shows the usable depth. |
| 12 | **The orientation shot** — the facade in direct sun | The site publishes each unit's facing (bahary / qibly). This proves it. |

### Additional, by type

- **Villa / ground floor with garden:** the garden from the house, the house
  from the garden, the roof, the parking.
- **Shop or café:** the frontage from across the street (frontage metres are
  the value), the interior from the door, the storage, the electrical board.
- **Under construction:** the site, the structure, the developer's site board
  with the delivery date legible.

## Technical

| | |
|---|---|
| Files | **Raw highest-resolution originals.** Not WhatsApp exports — WhatsApp recompresses to roughly a tenth of the data and it cannot be recovered. |
| Minimum | 3000px on the long edge |
| Orientation | Landscape for rooms. The card mask is 4:3. |
| Lens | Wide, but **not fisheye.** A 12 m² bedroom photographed to look like 20 m² gets found out at the viewing and costs the deal. |
| Light | Shoot rooms in daylight with the lights on. Curtains open. |
| Tripod | Camera at about 1.4 m, level. A tilted horizon reads as amateur instantly. |
| Prep | Clear the surfaces. No bins, no shoes, no personal photographs, no agent in the mirror. |

## Grade

**One colour treatment across the whole library.** Pick it on the first shoot
and apply it to everything after — slightly cool, neutral whites, no heavy
contrast, no orange-and-teal. The point is that a grid of twenty units from
twenty different flats reads as *one agency's work*. Mixed grades are why most
Egyptian property sites look like a folder rather than a portfolio.

## Day / evening

Where both exist, shoot the reception and the main view **twice** — daylight
and after Maghrib, tripod not moved between frames. The schema already carries
`nightShots` as day/night pairs. Light is what people are buying, and
compounds in October and Zayed are visited after dark.

## Floor plans

Separate from photography and just as important.

The site's signature effect is the plan drawing itself, and it is generated
from **measurements, not images**: each room is a rectangle in centimetres, and
the area printed on the drawing is computed from it.

So what is needed per unit is not a scan. It is:

- room-by-room internal dimensions (width × depth, in cm)
- which wall each door is in
- the unit's facing direction (N / NE / E / …)

A tape measure and a notebook produce better input than a photographed
developer brochure. Where a proper CAD or vector plan exists, send it — it can
be used directly.
